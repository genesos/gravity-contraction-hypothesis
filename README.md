# Gravity Contraction Hypothesis

## Overview

This repository presents a novel interpretation of gravity: **Gravity is not an interaction between objects, but a local contraction at each point in space.**

## The Hypothesis

Traditional gravitational theory describes gravity as a force or interaction between massive objects. However, the Gravity Contraction Hypothesis proposes a fundamentally different view:

- **Local Phenomenon**: Gravity arises from localized contractions in the fabric of space itself at each point
- **No Action at a Distance**: Rather than objects "pulling" on each other, each mass creates a local spatial contraction around it
- **Independent Contractions**: Each point in space contracts independently based on local conditions
- **Emergent Behavior**: What we observe as gravitational attraction is the emergent effect of accumulated local contractions

## Key Principles

### 1. Local Space Contraction
At each point in space, the presence of mass-energy causes a local contraction. This contraction is:
- Proportional to the local energy density
- Independent of other points (though coupled through the geometry)
- A fundamental property of spacetime geometry

### 2. No Direct Interaction
Objects do not "interact" gravitationally. Instead:
- Each mass creates its own local spatial contractions
- Objects follow geodesics through the contracted space
- Apparent "attraction" is the result of motion through contracted geometry

### 3. Field Independence
Unlike field theories where particles exchange force carriers:
- No gravitons are required
- No force is transmitted between objects
- The contraction is a geometric property, not a force field

## Mathematical Framework

The local contraction at a point can be described by a metric tensor that encodes the contraction rate:

```
g_μν(x) = η_μν + h_μν(x)
```

Where:
- `η_μν` is the flat spacetime metric
- `h_μν(x)` represents the local contraction tensor at point x
- The contraction is determined solely by local mass-energy density

## Implications

1. **Philosophical**: Removes the need for "spooky action at a distance"
2. **Computational**: Each point can be computed independently in parallel
3. **Conceptual**: Simplifies the understanding of gravity as purely geometric
4. **Experimental**: Suggests new tests focusing on local measurements rather than distant interactions

## Comparison with General Relativity

While General Relativity describes gravity as curved spacetime, the Gravity Contraction Hypothesis emphasizes:
- **Locality**: Each point contracts independently
- **No Interaction**: Objects don't interact; they respond to local geometry
- **Contraction vs Curvature**: Framing in terms of spatial contraction rather than spacetime curvature
